How it works

Three ways in, one clean export.

However the design reaches you — a screenshot in your downloads, a link to a live site, or a single component you want to lift — WebNail reads the pixels and writes the markup.

01 — Capture

Upload, link, or select

Drag in a screenshot, paste a URL to crawl its pages, or open the WebNail extension and drag a selection box around any element on a live page.

02 — Read

Layout gets understood

WebNail maps spacing, type, color, and structure from the image or DOM — not a guess at what it might be, but what's actually there, pixel for pixel.

03 — Ship

Get real, editable code

Clean HTML/CSS or React comes back, ready to drop into a project, tweak, or hand to a teammate — no proprietary format to fight with.

Browser extension

Select right on the page you're looking at.

Open the WebNail extension on any site and it shows you that page immediately — no re-uploading, no re-pasting the link. Drag a box around a header, a card, or the whole layout, and export it on the spot.

Drag-select anything

Box-select a single component or the entire viewport — the selection snaps to real DOM boundaries as you drag.

Works on the live page

Reads the page you're already on, so fonts, real content, and computed styles come through — not just a flat screenshot.

Nothing leaves your browser

Selections are captured and sent straight to your account for generation — no third parties in between.
